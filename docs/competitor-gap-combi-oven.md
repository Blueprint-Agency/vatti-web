- ❌ copy for the range sold: the live copy was written for ten models and the comment above it
  still says "Ten models". Six are retired (`retired-products-2026-08.sql`): VA01, VA03, VA04,
  Z4501, O755P, O7549. The page names five of them as if on sale: the VA01 as the freestanding
  option, the Z4501 as "the steam-only model here", the VA03 for humidity grades and ceramic
  coating, the O755P for its door, and "all three built-in ovens" for an A+ rating two of them no
  longer back. The range is four: O7559 (75L air-fryer oven), VA05 and VA06 (70L combis), M626
  (25L microwave). This is a defect before it is a gap.
- ✅ local dishes: VA05 has steam rice, fermentation, yogurt, keep warm and defrost; VA06 adds
  three stew modes (porridge, meat, soup) and a multi-dish steam mode. No competitor page frames a
  combi around what a Malaysian kitchen steams.
- ✅ steam level: VA05 low, mid and high steam; VA06 three grades of humidity.
- ✅ air frying: all three ovens air fry (O7559 with its own rack, VA05, VA06 with two modes).
- ❌ home vs restaurant: nobody says it, us included. One clause fixes it.
- ⚠️ capacity: the ovens are 70 and 75L; the old copy's "25 to 75L" counted the microwave as an
  oven. Honest version: three full-size ovens and a microwave.
- ❌ steam-only and compact: retired with the Z4501 and the smaller ovens. Say what the range is;
  do not write around it.

## Decisions

Gap we own: steam set to Malaysian food. Three steam levels, steam rice, porridge and stew modes,
proving and yogurt, in a full-size oven that also air fries. That is a cook we can name: a
household that steams as often as it bakes.

Build:

1. **Rewrite the six guides and six reasons for the four models on sale.** Remove every named
   retired model. Each claim says which model it is true of.
2. **Fix FAQ 3 and FAQ 7**, which name the Z4501 and miss the VA06's 1.3L tank. Keep the other
   eight close to the live wording: the live page already emits FAQPage schema.
