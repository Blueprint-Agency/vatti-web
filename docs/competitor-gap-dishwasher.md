## Why this page, and what could not be measured

The workbook (21 August) put this page against Fotile as a head-to-head: "Fotile #5, Vatti #8.9"
for `dishwasher malaysia`. Neither figure survives a fresh look:

- **Search Console was not readable.** The connected Google account is `siteUnverifiedUser` on
  `https://vattimalaysia.com/` and every query returns 403. The 8.9 is a GSC average position from
  the workbook and cannot be re-checked until someone with owner access grants full user rights.
  This blocks every GSC-based figure in this brief, not only this one.
- **Ubersuggest SERP, Malaysia locId 2458, English, data dated 2026-09-19:** vattimalaysia.com is
  not in the top 23 for `dishwasher malaysia`. Nor is Fotile's category page; Fotile's only entry
  is a blog post, "Best Dishwasher for Modern Homes in Malaysia", at 11 and 20.

So the framing changes. This is not a page fight with Fotile. It is a page that does not yet rank
for its head term, in a SERP where the one direct competitor does not rank with its category page
either.

Notes on the Us column:

- ✅ capacity: DWBB7 holds 17 place settings, DWID3 20 plus a 2-setting fruit and vegetable
  basket (`product_spec`). Electrolux tops out at 14, Toshiba at 15. This is the one gap every
  competitor misses and that we hold outright, and the page does not lead with it.
- ✅ wok cookware and ⚠️ the rest: the current copy does frame the 75°C wash around cooking oil and
  the 168-hour ventilation around a machine left shut and damp. Those are the right angles. They
  are written for one model.
- ❌ copy for the range sold: `category-content.sql` was written when the DWBB7 was the only
  dishwasher. The first guide is headed "One machine, seventeen settings"; the comments call it a
  one-model page. The DWID3 has since shipped (two colourways, collapsed to one model on the
  page), with 20+2 settings, a 55,000 Pa variable-pressure pump, smart detergent dosing, Wi-Fi and
  a 105°C dry. The page understates the bigger machine and misstates its own range. This is a
  defect before it is a gap.
- ⚠️ "is it worth it": FAQ 5 ("better than hand washing?") half-answers it, and two articles answer
  it fully (`buying-guide/is-a-dishwasher-worth-it`, `buying-guide/is-a-dishwasher-necessary`).
  Neither is surfaced as the answer on the page the head-term searcher lands on.
- ❌ sizing table: both VATTI machines are large-household machines. A sizing table that is honest
  says so: for 1 to 2 people, VATTI does not make the right size. That is a ❌ we cannot fix with
  copy, and the compensating move is to say who the machines are for rather than hide it.
- ❌ running cost, noise: `product_spec` has no kWh, litres or dB for either dishwasher. The
  catalogue may. Do not write "costs less to run than it looks" (guide 5, FAQ 3, FAQ 8 all do)
  without a figure behind it; that is the claim the forum threads exist to test.
- ❌ price, instalment: same position as the hood page. No price data in `data/sql`, and every
  conversion path ends in WhatsApp by the owner's decision.
- ✅ warranty: 2 years, on the page since 2026-10-03 (`warrantyLine`). Parity with Electrolux's 24
  months, well short of Bosch's 10-year anti-rust. Stated, not a selling point.

## Decisions

Gap we own: capacity. Every competitor tops out at 15 or below and both VATTI machines are above
it. Paired with oily cookware and a machine that stays dry in a humid kitchen, that is a buyer we
can name: a large household that cooks with oil every day. The page should be written for that
buyer and say so plainly, including that it is not the machine for a couple in a studio.

Build:

1. **Rewrite the six guides, six reasons and the meta description for a two-model range.** Lead
   with capacity: 17 and 20 place settings, and what that means in meals. Retire "One machine,
   seventeen settings". Every figure from `product_spec`; nothing about the DWID3 that its spec
   rows do not carry.
2. **Answer "is it worth having one" on the page**, as the first FAQ, in the page's own figures
   (75°C, capacity, time), linking to `buying-guide/is-a-dishwasher-worth-it` for the long answer.
   This is the question three of the top ten results exist to ask.
3. **Say who the machines are for**, as a guide block: a household of four or more, a table of
   guests, a kitchen that cooks with oil daily. One sentence that a smaller household should look
   at a compact machine, without naming one. This is the honest version of Electrolux's table.
4. **Strip the unsupported running-cost claims** from guide 5 and FAQs 3, 8 and 9, or replace them
   with figures once the client supplies them (see Ask). "Uses less water than a running tap" is a
   general truth that can stay; "costs less than it looks" without a number cannot.
5. **Point the dishwasher articles at the category page** with a dishwasher anchor, the same move
   as the hood cluster. Eight published articles; check each links `/dishwasher-in-malaysia/`
   rather than a redirecting path.

Skip:

- A listicle. The SERP's listicle is one brand's stock list from 2024; its strength is the figures,
  which build 1 and Ask 2 cover on our own page.
- Countertop or sink formats. We do not make them. Say so, do not write around it.
- Copying Electrolux's types-size-consider-cost-why block. It is the shape of a page that already
  ranks second; matching it is how we rank eleventh.
- Naming any competitor on the page.

Ask the client:

1. **Price band** for each model, even "from RM X". Same ask as the hood page; one answer unlocks
   both and schedule item 6.
2. **Energy (kWh), water (litres) and noise (dB) per cycle** for DWBB7 and DWID3, from the
   catalogue or spec sheet. With these, running cost per wash becomes a figure no brand page in
   the top ten prints, and the forum question gets a direct answer.
3. **Installation**: does the dealer plumb it in, and is that included or quoted?
4. **Search Console access**: owner to add the agency's Google account as a full user on
   `https://vattimalaysia.com/` (currently unverified). Without it the schedule's progress cannot
   be measured.

Dead results, so nobody re-proposes them:

- `dishwasher` (5,400/month) is a retailer and marketplace term, and also catches detergent,
  Sunlight and job searches. Not winnable by a single-brand category page.
- `tabletop dishwasher` (210/month): VATTI has no product for it.
- "Fotile #5 on dishwasher malaysia": not true as of 2026-09-19. Fotile ranks with a blog post at
  11; its category page is not in the top 23. The head-to-head framing in the schedule does not
  hold for this page.

## Client answers (2026-10-03)

- Price band: **no.** No price figure goes on the page.
- Energy, water and noise per cycle: **no.** The rewrite removed every running-cost claim it
  could not back with a figure, and makes none of its own.
- Installation: **not to be mentioned.** The old guide's "have the dealer install it" went with
  the rewrite.

So three rows of the gap table stay ❌ by decision rather than by missing data. The compensating
move for all three is the one the rewrite leads with: capacity, wok oil and a machine that stays
dry, all of which are measured on the spec sheet.
